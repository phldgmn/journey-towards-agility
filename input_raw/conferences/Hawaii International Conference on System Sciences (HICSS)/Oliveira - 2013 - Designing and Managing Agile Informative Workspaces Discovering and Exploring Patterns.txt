 







An Informative Workspace (IW) consists in the use
of the workplace for displaying and using information
on agile environments. Some authors have provided
guidelines for designing and maintaining IWs, but few
are based on more formal studies. This research aims
to help understanding how to optimize the use of IWs
on agile software development teams. We performed a
study using a mixed method research approach based
on action research, survey 
and interviews. Our results
pointed out seven heuri
stics for designing and
managing IWs, and concepts ex
plaining "Why'', "How''
and "When'' they apply. These heuristics and related
concepts provide effective 
hints on how to use an IW.
