 Abstract
 Software development projects have evolved over time and newer
 methods,
 Agile software 
development methods are changing the ways software is developed. Agile methods are based on a set 
of values and activities that reflect the values and teaching Agile methods should discuss both the 
values as well as the methods themselves. 
This teaching case describes a successful
 Agile
 software 
product development project. The case describes how Agile
 software methods, communication tools 
and prototypes were used to ensure efficient communication and collaboration amongst stakeholders.
 The case illustrates how artefacts and tools can be used to clarify the project goals and 
maintain
 effective
 customer
 collaboration. The case also illustrates how the artefacts and tools can be used to 
mitigate risks of communicational breakdowns. 
 The teaching notes suggest that the case can be used 
to teach 
the concep
ts of 
Agile software development
, stakeholder theory
 and concept of b
oundary 
objects. 
 Keywords:

, Case study, Stakeholder theory, Boundary objects

 The concept of boundary objects will aid students when conducting a deeper 
analysis of the tools and 
artefacts used in the project. 
Boundary objects are objects that are used to convey information across 
boundaries of groups or organization
. Boundary objects are objects that reside in the boundaries of 
organisations and people. T
hey are plastic and adaptable to local needs but also robust enough to 
maintain common identity (Star &
 Griesemer 1989). 
Boundary objects allow different groups to work 
together without consensus (Star 2010). 
  Students should discuss where the boundaries 
of the projects can be drawn in this case study. The 
boundary can be drawn between the Agile development team and the non
-Agile Escapade 
stakeholders but other possible boundaries can be also identified.
 Students should justify why they 
assume a boundary m
ight exist.
