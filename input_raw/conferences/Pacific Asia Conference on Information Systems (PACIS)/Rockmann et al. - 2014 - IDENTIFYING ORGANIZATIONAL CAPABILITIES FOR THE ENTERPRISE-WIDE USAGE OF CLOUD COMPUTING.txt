  Abstract
 Cloud computing (CC)

receive

Especially the public cloud model promises several

propos

 Organizational c
ulture
 plays a key role in the assimilation of information technology 
(see e.g. 
Leidner and Kayworth 2006
). Ruppel and Harrington (2001)
 concluded that intranet adoption is much 
more likely to succeed in cultu
res with emphasized flexibility and innovation values. Thus, the 
definition of 
Powell and Dent

define organizational flexibility 
as a Ò

and welcomes opport
unities to apply new IT developments
Ó. Subsequently, we propose that a climate 
that is supportive of using CC resources and services fosters experimentation with CC resources and, 
hence, contributes to diffusion and infusion of CC.

 CONTRIBUTION
 We expect to identify d

. For example, using

. As suggested by the

expe
cted patterns are intended to serve

. The study 
will provide
 novel insights on 
how CC can be fully

 the

exploit 
public
 CC resources
, as well as
 to guide
