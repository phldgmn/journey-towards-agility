Abstract:  

In recent years a host of new agile software develo
pment methodologies have been introduced. 
These methodologies emphasize people over process, 
software over documentation, customer 
collaboration over contract negotiation and respond
ing to change over following a plan. They are 
quite different from the traditional process orient
ed approaches. One major implication of these 
differences is the need for a less rigid and formal
 approach to project management. This paper 
describes leadership characteristics that are condu
cive to agile software development 
environments and maps these characteristics to Chur
chman's five modes of inquiry. With agile's 
focus on people over processes, it is critical to a
 project's success to have the right types of 
people "leading the charge". By mapping desirable c
haracteristics of agile leaders to specific 
inquiry modes, the goal of this paper is to provide
 insight into the types of managers that are 
most likely to be successful in an agile environmen
t.
