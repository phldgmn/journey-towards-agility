Sample Size
: The reliability of the factor analysis is 
dependent on sample size. (Kass et al. 1979) recomm

p to total 
300. (Tabachnick et al. 2001) agreed that it is com

olution. 
Another way is to measure the Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin me
asure

tio of 
the squared correlation between variables to the sq

partial correlation between variables. According to
 (Kaiser

able, 
values between .5 and .7 are mediocre, values betwe

.8 are good, values between .8 and .9 are great, an
d values 
above .9 are superb. With our sample size and a KMO

Factor Scores

There are several techniques for calculating factor
