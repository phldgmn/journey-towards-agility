 Background:
 Agile development heavily relies on effective 
communication and coordination. Large

 Aim:
 We aim to understand

questions: Who supports the teams in their daily work? How often

external

Are

 We use the data from 10 teams from two large
-scale

  Results:
 In this paper, we identify a number of governance roles 
supporting the teams, and discuss their importance
, knowledge

 and contact availability 
in the context of lar
ge-scale distributed projects.
  Conclusion:
 We conclude that teams interact with a large number

Although the

gile
