 10 most Powerful Ideas in Software Engineering 
Steve McConnell ConstruxAbstractAt software engineering™s 
40th birthday, do we know which software engineering ideas matter most? 
In this talk, award-winning author Steve McConnell 

identifies 10 of the most pow
erful ideas in software engineering. McConnell explains how the 10 ideas 
form the foundation for effective software 

development, and he shows how practices ranging 
from the waterfall model to extreme programming 
measure up. He uses these key ideas to explain which 

currently popular software engineering practices will 
withstand the test of time, and which are fleeting fads. BiographySteve McConnell is best known as author of Software Estimation: Demystifying the Black Art,Code Complete 2nd Edition,Rapid Development, and other software industry classics. His first two books won Software Development magazine's Jolt Excellence 
award for best programming books of their years. 
In 1998, readers of Software Development magazine 
named Steve one of the three most influential people in 

the software industry along with Bill Gates and Linus 
Torvalds. He is past Editor in Chief of IEEE Software 

magazine and past Chair of the IEEE Professional 

Practices Committee. 
Steve currently works as CEO and Chief Software 
Engineer at Construx Software (www.construx.com), 
where he personally works with a few select clients to 

improve their software development practices, and also 

oversees the rest of Construx's work with many other 

companies. Steve can be reached at 

stevemcc@construx.com.
