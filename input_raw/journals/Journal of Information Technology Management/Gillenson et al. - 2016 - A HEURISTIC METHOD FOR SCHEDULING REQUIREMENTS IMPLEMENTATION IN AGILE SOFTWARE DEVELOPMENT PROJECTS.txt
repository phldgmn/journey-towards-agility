One of the
 
challenges of successfully developing software using an agile software development methodology is the 
determination of which requirements or features to implement in which of the agile iterations. These decisions involve prior

ties, risk, development cost

the possibility of software failur

n-
tinue. With a sample project, this paper presents a heuristic method for scheduling requirements implementation in agile d
e-
velopment iterations, taking into accou
nt all of the aforementioned consider
a
tions.
