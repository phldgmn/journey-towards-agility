 Abstract
 Agile teams strive to balance short term feature development with longer term quality concerns.  These 
evolutionary approaches often hit a "complexity wall" from the cumulative effects of unplanned 
changes, resulting in unreliable
, poorly performing software. Consequently, there is renewed focus on 
approaches to address architectural concerns within the Agile community. 
 W
e present 
an analysis of 
quality attribute concerns from 
15 years of 
Architecture Trade
-off Analysis Method 
data
, gathered from
 31 projects.  We found that modifiability is the 
dominant
 c
oncern across all project types;
 additionally 
there was considerable focus on performance, availability, and interoperability. For information 

technology projects, a relatively new 
quality
Šdeployability
Šhas emerged as a key concern.  Our results 
provide insights for 
Agile 
teams allocating architecture
-related tasks to iterations. For example they can 
use these results to create checklists for release planning or retrospectives to hel
p assess whether a 
given quality should be addressed to support future needs.

Sidebar: The ATAM 
 The ATAM is a method for anal
yzing software and system 
architectures with respect to quality goals [7]. The ATAM has a 
long pedigree
Šit has been used in many government and 
commercial organizations for more than 10 years
Šand has a 
substantial amount of supporting documentation in the 
form of 
books, papers, and training courses.  The ATAM is traditionally 
enacted in two phases.  Phase 1 is focused on eliciting 
information about the architecture from the architecture team. 
Phase 2 is focused on eliciting project stakeholder needs. The 
AT
AM includes many component techniques that have been 
developed and refined over the years such as 
Quality Attribute 
Scenario (QAS) analysis 
which is a method for capturing 
architecturally focused requirements from stakeholders.
 QAS 
follow a structured form
at defined in 6 parts: 1. an explicit 
stimulus

stimulus source
, an explicit 
response
,3.  a 
response 
measure

environment
, and 5. the 
artifact
 affected by the 
scenario.. The 
utility tree
 is a hierarchically organized affinity 
grouping of quality attribute concerns/ scenarios derived from a 

particular project [7].  
Quality attribute 
tactics
 are architectural 
design primitives that ATAM evaluators look for in the 
architecture, to quickly as
sess the strategies employed by the 
architects [7].  These component techniques aid the ATAM team 
in repeatable eliciting and analyzing QA information.
