ML#1: Don™t Underpower 
 Infrastructure in Remote Locations
We heard astonishing stories about how

With distributed teams, the mere

ML#2: Don™t Hire Testers 
 12 Hours Away to Save Money
Managers too often selected testers

jrothman.com/blog/mpd/2010/03/

wage-cost-and-project-labor-cost.

Distributing work to reduce cost

team is being distributed and ensure

CL#1: Different Isn™t Wrong,

People are different, and distributed

CL#2: Sharing Food

One proven and powerful technique for

When you bring people together, do

You can make geographically distrib
