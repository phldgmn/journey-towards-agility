STRATEGIC IMPACT
These systems are gaining stra

At the same time, they must be

Additional factors complicating

such as making CEOs and CTOs/CIOs

in the software systems that run the 
company and its communication 
with its operational environment and 
ecosystems.
Binding oversight
On the one hand, software pro
-duction, maintenance, and evolution 
become increasingly distributed and 
delegated to third parties. On the 
other hand, companies feel a grow
-ing need to tightly control their own 
business and its strategies, processes, 
rules, and regulations. This requires 
application and business experts to 
continuously oversee and steer the 
scenario from their unique perspec
-tive. State-of-the-art methods and

Aspect orientation organizes/

the reuse of the so-called aspect

each aspect delegating the cor
-rect positioning to a separately

Aspect orientation is a power
