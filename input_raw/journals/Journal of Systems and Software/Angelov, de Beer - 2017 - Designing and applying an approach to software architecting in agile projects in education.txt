 an approach

 two

 The strategies

 to be mostly

 Using our approach,

 They
 appreciate
 the upfront

 requires

 It has been
 applied

 do not

 ities performed
 in projects.
 Future
 work
 needs
 to look into

 unsavvy
 POs and the approaches

 can follow
 to alleviate

 has also to be adapted

 POs. Another

 for future

 is inspired

 and agile development

 One possible future
 direc-
 tion for addressing

 tool-

 of the architecture

 Agile methods
 introduce
 challenges
 to applying
 software
 archi-
 tecting
 in education,
 but they
 also offer

 inﬂuential learning techniques.
 The iterative

 Our approach
 is targeted
