 2. Background
 2.1.

 In 1970,

 ) ( Brereton

 agile methods offer

 recommendations

 development
 is in- cluded

 is presented

 and requirements

 The naturally

 development
 cycle

 development

 iterative
 prac-
 tices are

 system

 the moti-
 vation

 Compared
 to the typical experienced
 beneﬁts
 of agile methods

 development,
 there
 is a change

 Accord-

 of the three
 cases, productivity
 and  ciency was
 not
 improved

 de- velopment.
 This may
 have
 been
 affected
 also by the short length of the observation

 development,
 team-
 work
 and communication were

 and communication will probably
 lead to bet-
 ter
 productivity
 and .
 In short term,
 the improved
 team-
 work
 and communication lead to better
 system-wide
 understand-

 need
 for documentation,
 and enabled

 interdependencies
 better
 into

 Dividing
 the work
 into
 iterations
 especially
 on the hardware
 side was
 experienced
  and all the tasks
 were
 not

 Utilization of agile methods
 in embedded
 system
 development
 challenged
 most
 the agile value
 of the working
 prod-
 uct:
 the focus shifted
 from
 the documentation
 to the work
 accom- plished,
 instead

 product.
 In the case studies
 it was
 noted,
 that the agile value
 of respect-
 ing individuals
 and interactions
 is a key for successful adoption
 of agile practices.
 The team
 –and
 each
 individual
 in it –is
 responsi-
 ble for the development
 of their own
 working
 methods.
 Obtaining
 the full beneﬁts

 in embedded
 system
