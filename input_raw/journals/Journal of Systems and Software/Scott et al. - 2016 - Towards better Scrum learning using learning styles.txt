 2. Background
 In recent
 years,

 have

 of learning styles
 A widely

 preferences

 porated

 and process

 First,
 previous
 evidence
 on the relationship
 between
 Scrum and learning styles
 in the context
 of an undergraduate
 software
 engineering course was
 found ( Scott
 et al., 2014a
 ). Second, we found that the processing
 dimension bore

 statistically

 ( Scott
 et al., 2013
 ). Third,
 FSLSM
 has been

 and Liu, 2009

 ( Feldman
 et al., 2014
 ). The FSLSM

 Table
 1 shows

 processing,
 and understanding,

 The perception
 dimension indicates

 nal) such

 (internal)

 The understanding

 or in ﬁts

 dimension indicates

 Finally,

 or auditory

 students

 students

 students

 students
