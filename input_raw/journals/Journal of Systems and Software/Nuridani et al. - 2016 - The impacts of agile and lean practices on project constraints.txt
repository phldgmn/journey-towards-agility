 2. Tertiary

 in software

 Software

 et al., 2004
 ) has its

 Kitchenham

 informed

 (3) improve

 (4) improve

 systematic
 approach

 et al., 2004

 uate
 and summarize the ﬁndings of all relevant
 individual
 studies,

 making the available

 more

 Centre
 for Reviews
 and Dissemination, 2009

 individual
 studies
 is expected

 a more
 reliable
 and precise
 estimate
 of the effectiveness
 of an intervention.
 In our tertiary
 study,
 an intervention
 is an Agile and Lean practice.
 When there
 are
 many
 secondary
 studies
 in an area
 such

 engineering, it is possible to perform a tertiary
 study
 ( Kitchenham
 and Charters,
 2007
 ). Kitchenham
 et al. (2009)

 Kitchenham
 et al. (2010)

 only

 SLRs perform evaluations

 MacDonell et al. (2010)
 performed

 study

 independent SLRs with a common research
