 2. Background
 Within a relatively
 short amount of time, an increasing
 number

 have
 adopted

 When consid- ering actions taking

 companies

 framework

 Backlog

 Product
 Backlog

 These items

 For
 each
 Sprint, a collection of Product
 Backlog

 consisting

 Collectively,

 Product
 Backlog

 work
 estimates
 for all of them. After

 mented

 team
 that executes

 Tools
 that are

 needed

 control,
 man- aging Backlog
 Items,
 testing

 The details
 of the tool

 Upon
 the completion
 of each
 Sprint, a complete
 system
 that can in principle be delivered

 In order
 to learn from
 each
 Sprint, a Retrospective

 of working
 could be improved.
 Only
 three
 roles
 are
 deﬁned
 in Scrum: the Product
 Owner is re-
 sponsible for managing the Product
 Backlog,
 the Team
 consists
 of developers
 responsible
 for executing
 the Sprint, and the duty of the Scrum Master
 is to eliminate
 any
 emerging
 impediment
 and enforce
 the Scrum process.
 Although the above
 ideals form a coherent
 framework
 for execut-
 ing development
 tasks,
 it is common that organizations

 them only
 partially
 or in a fashion
 where
 some of the practices
 are
 totally
 omitted.
 Omitting some Scrum practices
 or changing
 them in a way
 that is not
 aligned
 with the Scrum guidelines is commonly
 referred
 to as ScrumBut [18]
 . Such
 misalignment has been
 blamed
 to be a source
 of missing many
 of the beneﬁts
 of Scrum [19]
 , although little scien- tiﬁc evidence
 regarding
 this has been
 presented.

 9. Conclusions Due to the rapid

 engineering, new

 It is only

 At this point, it is possible to gather

 In this paper,

 introduced

 Scrum is deployed

 no- table

 A central

 The list

 gives

 This work

 Indeed,

 Finding

 dence calls for further research

 set

 The results
 of our work
 can be exploited

 Scrum into

 to pay

 Scrum deviations

 have
 unpleasant
 consequences.
 The list
 can also be used

 and to pro-
 vide guidance and practice
